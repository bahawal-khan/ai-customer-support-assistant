# NovaCart Customer Support Policy

**Document:** Customer Support & Service Policy  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** August 2026

## 1. Delivery Policy

NovaCart aims to deliver standard orders within **3–5 business days** after shipment. Express orders normally arrive within **1–2 business days**.

Delivery estimates are not guaranteed unless an order is explicitly marked as a guaranteed delivery service. Weekends and public holidays may extend delivery times.

### Delayed Orders

If a standard order has not arrived within **2 business days after the estimated delivery date**, the customer may report the delay through **Order History → Report a Delivery Problem**.

NovaCart will review the tracking information and, when appropriate, contact the carrier.

### Package Marked as Delivered but Not Received

Customers should first check the porch, mailroom, side entrance, package room, nearby safe locations, and with household members or neighbors.

Some carriers may scan a package as delivered shortly before the physical delivery. Customers should wait until the **end of the delivery day** before reporting a missing delivered package.

If the package is still missing after **24 hours**, the customer should submit a delivery problem report. NovaCart may open a carrier investigation.

If the carrier confirms that the package was lost, NovaCart may provide a **free replacement or a full refund**, depending on the customer's preference and item availability.

## 2. Damaged or Defective Items

If an item arrives visibly damaged or defective, the customer should take photographs of the package and product when practical.

The issue should be reported through **Order History → Report a Delivery Problem**.

Eligible damaged or defective items qualify for a **free replacement or full refund**. Customers are not required to pay return shipping for an item that arrived damaged or defective.

## 3. Wrong Item

If NovaCart ships the wrong item, the customer should contact support and provide the order number and a description of the item received.

NovaCart will arrange a correction. When a return is required, NovaCart provides return instructions and covers the applicable return shipping cost.

## 4. Refund Policy

A customer may qualify for a full refund when:

- The item arrives damaged or defective.
- The item is materially different from its description.
- The customer returns an unused item in its original packaging within **30 days of delivery**.
- The order was cancelled before shipment.
- The carrier confirms that the package was lost and NovaCart cannot provide a replacement within the applicable service period.
- NovaCart shipped the wrong item.

Refund eligibility can depend on the order status and the specific circumstances of the case.

### Refund Processing

Once a refund is approved, NovaCart sends the refund to the original payment method.

The time required for the refund to appear depends on the payment provider. NovaCart does not control the processing time after the refund has been issued.

## 5. Order Cancellation

An order may be cancelled before it enters the shipping process.

Once an order has shipped, cancellation may no longer be possible. In that situation, the customer may need to use the applicable return process after delivery.

Customers should submit cancellation requests as soon as possible through their account.

## 6. Returns

Eligible returns must normally be requested within **30 days of delivery**.

Returned products should be unused and, when applicable, in their original packaging.

Some products may have additional return restrictions. The specific restrictions shown for the product or order take precedence over this general policy.

## 7. Customer Support Response Priorities

NovaCart prioritizes cases as follows:

### High Priority

Examples include:

- Payment or account security concerns.
- A potentially fraudulent transaction.
- A serious safety issue involving a product.
- Multiple failed delivery attempts where an urgent resolution is required.

### Medium Priority

Examples include:

- Significantly delayed deliveries.
- Damaged or defective products.
- Wrong items received.
- Refund issues that have exceeded the normal processing period.

### Low Priority

Examples include:

- General product questions.
- Positive feedback.
- Routine delivery questions.
- Requests for general information.

## 8. Important Support Rule

Customer-support responses must be based on the applicable NovaCart policy and verified order information.

If the available policy does not contain an answer, support should **not invent a policy, deadline, fee, refund, replacement, or guarantee**. The customer should instead be informed that the case requires further review.

Personal customer information, payment details, passwords, and authentication codes must never be requested unnecessarily or included in public support responses.

## 9. Example Support Questions

The following questions can be answered using this policy:

- How long does standard delivery take?
- My package says delivered but I cannot find it. What should I do?
- Can I get a refund for a damaged item?
- What should I do if I received the wrong product?
- How long do I have to return an item?
- Can I cancel my order after it has shipped?
- What issues are considered high priority?
- What happens if my package is confirmed lost?

## 10. Policy Precedence

This document provides general customer-support guidance. If an order-specific policy, product-specific restriction, or legally required rule differs from this document, the applicable order-specific or higher-priority rule takes precedence.
