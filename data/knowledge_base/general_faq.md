# General FAQ

> **Fictional demo content.** NovaCart is not a real company. This document
> is a synthetic support-knowledge article created for a portfolio project
> and does not represent the policy of any real business.

## Overview

This is the general starting-point FAQ for NovaCart, an online retailer of
electronics and smart-home/networking products. It covers account basics,
orders, membership, and points customers to the more detailed policy
documents for specific situations.

## About NovaCart

NovaCart (fictional, demo only) sells consumer electronics, smart-home
devices, and home networking equipment such as routers and mesh Wi-Fi
systems, and provides setup/technical support for the products it sells.

## Creating and Managing an Account

- An account is required to place an order, though guest checkout is
  available for one-time purchases without saving details.
- Sign-up requires an email address and password; a confirmation email is
  sent to verify the address.
- Profile details (name, addresses, saved payment methods) can be managed
  from **Account → Profile**.
- For password resets, lost 2FA access, or suspected account compromise,
  see `account_recovery.md`.

## Placing an Order

1. Add items to the cart from any product page.
2. Go to **Cart → Checkout**.
3. Enter or select a shipping address, choose a shipping speed, and select
   a payment method.
4. Review the order total, including tax and shipping, and confirm.
5. An order confirmation is shown on-screen and emailed immediately.

Orders can be edited (address, shipping speed) for a short window before
processing begins — see `cancellation_policy.md` for exact timing.

## Checking Order Status

**Order History** shows every order's current stage: Placed, Processing,
Shipped, or Delivered, along with tracking information once available. See
`delivery_faq.md` for shipping-specific questions.

## NovaCart Plus Membership

NovaCart Plus is an optional paid membership offering:

- Free Standard and Express shipping on eligible orders.
- Free return shipping labels regardless of return reason (see
  `refund_policy.md`).
- Early access to sales events.
- Priority scheduling for installation appointments.

Membership can be started, paused, or cancelled at any time; see
`cancellation_policy.md` for how proration works.

## Product Questions Before Buying

- Full specifications, compatibility notes, and customer photos/reviews
  are listed on each product page.
- The **Ask a Question** feature on a product page routes pre-purchase
  questions to support or shows answers other customers received
  previously.
- NovaCart does not guarantee compatibility with products not sold by
  NovaCart (e.g., third-party smart-home hubs); compatibility notes on the
  product page reflect testing with common third-party systems where
  applicable.

## Price Matching and Price Drops

- If the price of an item drops within 7 days of a customer's purchase,
  NovaCart automatically refunds the difference as store credit; no
  request is needed.
- NovaCart does not price-match other retailers.
- Limited-time promotional prices and clearance items are excluded from
  the 7-day price-drop guarantee, and this exclusion is noted on the
  product page.

## Contacting Support

- **In-app chat**: fastest option for most questions, available from
  **Support → Chat With Us**.
- **Complaint/issue form**: for detailed written issues, available from
  **Support → Report an Issue**; this routes automatically based on the
  category selected (refund, delivery, technical, billing, account,
  other).
- **Phone support**: available for NovaCart Plus members and for
  safety-related issues, listed in **Support → Contact Options**.

Support does not require a customer to already know which policy applies
— describing the issue in plain language is enough for it to be routed
correctly.

## Product Categories NovaCart Sells

- Smart-home devices (plugs, speakers, sensors, cameras).
- Home networking equipment (routers, mesh systems, extenders).
- Streaming and entertainment devices.
- Accessories (cables, chargers, mounts).

Support documentation for the networking category is split into
`technical_support.md` (general device troubleshooting) and
`router_troubleshooting.md` (router- and Wi-Fi-specific troubleshooting).

## Business and Bulk Orders

NovaCart offers a separate business account type for bulk purchases,
invoicing, and dedicated account management. Business accounts have
different payment terms (see `payment_faq.md` for the bank-transfer
option) but the same product warranty terms as consumer accounts.

## Accessibility

NovaCart's website and app aim to follow common accessibility guidelines,
including keyboard navigation, screen-reader labeling, and adjustable text
size. Accessibility issues can be reported through **Support → Report an
Issue** under the "other" category, and are prioritized as a distinct
internal track from general bug reports.

## Notifications and Communication Preferences

NovaCart sends order confirmations, shipping updates, and security alerts
(password changes, new sign-ins) by email regardless of notification
preferences, since these are considered account-critical. Marketing
emails, promotional push notifications, and SMS updates can be turned off
individually from **Account → Notification Preferences** without affecting
critical order or security notifications.

## Returning Customer vs. Guest Checkout

Customers can switch between guest checkout and a full account at any
time; there is no penalty or restriction for using guest checkout
repeatedly. However, order history, saved payment methods, and NovaCart
Plus membership all require a full account, since they depend on
persistent account data that a one-time guest checkout does not create.

## Data and Privacy Basics

- Customers can view and download their stored order and account data from
  **Account → Privacy → Download My Data**.
- Account deletion is covered in `account_recovery.md`.
- NovaCart does not sell customer data to third parties for advertising;
  data shared with carriers and payment processors is limited to what is
  necessary to fulfill and pay for orders.

## Worked Examples

**Example 1 — New customer, first order.** A first-time visitor browses
routers, uses guest checkout to buy one without creating an account, and
later wants to track it. Because they checked out as a guest, they use the
tracking link emailed at shipment rather than an account-based Order
History page; creating an account later does not automatically retroactively
attach a guest order unless the same email is used and the order is
manually linked through support.

**Example 2 — Deciding between support channels.** A customer isn't sure
whether their issue is "big enough" for support. Any issue can be
described through **Support → Report an Issue** in plain language; the
system routes it based on category, so the customer never needs to
self-diagnose which policy document applies before reaching out.

**Example 3 — Price drop after purchase.** A customer buys a streaming
device for $80, and four days later it drops to $65 as part of a
non-promotional, non-clearance price adjustment. Because this is within
the 7-day window and the item isn't excluded, the $15 difference is
automatically credited as store credit without the customer needing to
notice or request it.

**Example 4 — Business account bulk order.** A small business orders 40
smart plugs for an office renovation using a business account with
bank-transfer payment terms. The product warranty terms are identical to a
consumer purchase (see `technical_support.md`), even though the payment
and invoicing process differs.

## Frequently Misunderstood Points

- **Guest checkout does not create an account** — order history for a
  guest order lives in the confirmation email and tracking link, not in a
  saved account.
- **The price-drop guarantee is automatic**, not something a customer
  needs to request within the 7-day window, but it does not apply to
  promotional or clearance pricing.
- **NovaCart does not price-match competitors** — the price-drop guarantee
  only compares against NovaCart's own later price, not other retailers.
- **Business accounts get different payment terms, not different product
  warranties** — hardware coverage is the same regardless of account
  type.
- **Downloading account data and deleting an account are separate
  actions** — requesting a data export does not start the deletion
  process, and vice versa.
