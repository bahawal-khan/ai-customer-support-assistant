# Delivery FAQ

> **Fictional demo content.** NovaCart is not a real company. This document
> is a synthetic support-knowledge article created for a portfolio project
> and does not represent the policy of any real business or carrier.

## Overview

This FAQ covers shipping speeds, tracking, delayed or missing packages,
delivery address changes, and damaged shipments. It applies to physical
orders shipped by NovaCart's carrier partners.

## Shipping Options

| Option | Typical transit time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 4–7 business days | Free over $50, otherwise $4.99 |
| Express | 2–3 business days | $9.99 |
| Next-Day | 1 business day (order by 2 PM local time) | $19.99 |
| NovaCart Plus members | Standard/Express free on eligible orders | Included in membership |

Transit times begin after the order leaves the warehouse (processing time
of up to 1 business day is separate from transit time).

## Tracking a Package

1. Go to **Order History** and select the order.
2. Select **Track Package** to see the carrier's tracking timeline.
3. A tracking link is also emailed once the order ships.

Tracking statuses generally follow this order: *Label Created → Picked Up
→ In Transit → Out for Delivery → Delivered*. Some carriers add
intermediate scan points (e.g., "Arrived at Facility") that do not
indicate a problem.

## My Package Says "Delivered" but I Don't Have It

1. Check around the delivery location — porches, mailrooms, side doors,
   and with neighbors or a building concierge, since carriers sometimes
   scan a package as delivered slightly before or after physically placing
   it.
2. Wait until the end of the delivery day; some carriers scan in bulk
   earlier than the physical drop-off.
3. If the package still cannot be found after 24 hours, report it through
   **Order History → Report a Delivery Problem**. NovaCart opens a carrier
   investigation.
4. If the carrier confirms the package is lost, NovaCart reships the item
   at no cost or issues a full refund per `refund_policy.md`, whichever
   the customer prefers.

## My Package Is Delayed

Delays can happen due to weather, carrier volume, customs (for
international orders), or an incorrect address. If a package has not
moved between tracking scans for more than 3 business days:

1. NovaCart automatically opens a carrier trace once this threshold is
   reached — customers do not need to request this manually, though they
   can still contact support for an update.
2. If the trace confirms the package is delayed but still moving, NovaCart
   provides an updated estimate.
3. If the trace confirms the package is lost or the delay exceeds 10
   business days past the original estimate, this is treated the same as
   a lost package (reship or refund).

## Changing a Delivery Address

- Before the order ships: the address can be edited from **Order History →
  Edit Order** (self-service), or updated instantly by support.
- After the order ships: the address usually cannot be changed mid-transit
  because carriers vary in whether they support redirection. Support can
  submit a redirection request to the carrier, but NovaCart cannot
  guarantee the carrier will honor it, and the customer should keep the
  original address available as a backup in case redirection fails.
- If a package is delivered to an old/incorrect address because the
  customer did not update it before shipping, NovaCart is not responsible
  for the lost package, but support will still help coordinate a
  recovery attempt with the carrier as a courtesy.

## Damaged Packages

- If a package arrives visibly damaged, the customer should take photos of
  the box and the item before opening further, if practical, and report it
  through **Order History → Report a Delivery Problem**.
- Damaged items are eligible for a free replacement or a full refund per
  `refund_policy.md` — the customer is never required to pay return
  shipping for a damaged item.
- Photos speed up processing but are not strictly required if the damage
  is described clearly; NovaCart does not want photo requirements to
  block a fast resolution.

## International Shipping

- Available to select countries, shown at checkout based on the shipping
  address entered.
- Import duties and taxes, where applicable, are calculated and collected
  at checkout so the customer is not surprised by charges on delivery,
  except in regions where the local carrier collects duties directly
  ("delivered duty unpaid"), which is clearly labeled at checkout.
- International transit times are typically 7–21 business days and are
  not covered by the domestic delay-trace threshold above; international
  traces begin after 15 business days of no scan movement.

## Multiple Packages, One Order

Orders with items from different warehouses or with different stock
availability may arrive in separate packages, each with its own tracking
number, at no extra cost to the customer. This is normal and is noted on
the order confirmation when it applies.

## Delivery Instructions and Signature Requirements

- Customers can add delivery instructions (e.g., "leave with concierge")
  during checkout or by editing the order before it ships.
- High-value orders (over $500) require a signature on delivery by
  default; customers may opt out of the signature requirement from
  **Order History → Delivery Preferences**, in which case NovaCart is not
  liable for a package marked delivered without signature.

## Refused or Undeliverable Packages

If a package is refused at delivery or returned to NovaCart as
undeliverable (e.g., after repeated failed delivery attempts), it is
treated as a customer-initiated return once it arrives back at the
warehouse, and a refund is processed per `refund_policy.md` minus original
shipping, unless the return was due to a NovaCart or carrier error.

## Escalation

Repeated delivery failures on the same account, carrier trace results that
contradict what the customer observed, or any delivery issue unresolved
after 10 business days past the original estimate should be escalated per
`escalation_policy.md`.

## Worked Examples

**Example 1 — "Delivered" but missing.** A customer's tracking shows
"Delivered" at 2:14 PM, but they were home all day and never heard a
knock. They check with a neighbor and the building's package room, find
nothing, and report it through **Report a Delivery Problem** the next
morning after confirming it's genuinely missing (not simply misplaced by
the carrier's scan-to-drop-off timing). NovaCart opens a carrier
investigation; if the carrier confirms the package is lost, the customer
chooses a free reship over a refund because they still want the item.

**Example 2 — Stalled tracking.** A package's last scan was 4 business
days ago at a regional sorting facility, with no movement since. Because
this exceeds the 3-business-day threshold, NovaCart has already opened an
automatic carrier trace before the customer even contacts support; when
they do reach out, support can share the trace status rather than starting
one from scratch.

**Example 3 — Damaged appliance.** A customer receives a large appliance
with a visibly dented corner. They photograph the box and item and report
it the same day. Because this is a damage claim, NovaCart offers a free
replacement with no return-shipping cost to the customer for the damaged
unit, rather than requiring a full return-and-repurchase cycle.

**Example 4 — International order with duties collected on delivery.** A
customer in a region where duties are collected by the carrier at the
door (not prepaid at NovaCart checkout) is surprised by a charge from the
carrier. Because this delivery-duty-unpaid arrangement is disclosed at
checkout for that region, the charge is expected and not a NovaCart
billing error, though support should still confirm the checkout disclosure
was shown correctly if the customer disputes seeing it.

## Frequently Misunderstood Points

- **A "Delivered" scan is not proof the customer has the package in hand**
  — carrier scan timing can occasionally lag or lead the physical
  drop-off by a short window.
- **Domestic and international delay thresholds differ** (3 business days
  vs. 15 business days) because international logistics naturally involve
  more transit steps and customs handling.
- **A carrier trace is often opened automatically** — customers don't
  always need to explicitly request one before it's already in progress.
- **Redirecting an in-transit package is a request to the carrier, not a
  guarantee** — NovaCart cannot force a carrier to honor a mid-transit
  address change.
- **Refusing delivery starts a return, not an instant refund** — the
  refund only begins once the carrier's return-to-sender scan is
  recorded.

## How Delivery Issues Relate to Other Policies

A delivery problem often becomes a different kind of case depending on the
outcome: a confirmed lost package becomes a refund or reship under
`refund_policy.md`; a delivery to a stale address the customer forgot to
update is a delivery issue but not a NovaCart error; and a pattern of
delivery problems on one account across multiple orders may warrant
escalation per `escalation_policy.md` even if each individual case was
resolved correctly, since the pattern itself may point to a
carrier-routing issue for that address.
