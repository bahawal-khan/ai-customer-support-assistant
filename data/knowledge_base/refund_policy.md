# Refund Policy

> **Fictional demo content.** NovaCart is not a real company. This document is
> a synthetic support-knowledge article created for a portfolio project. It
> does not describe the policy of any real business and must not be treated
> as legal or financial advice.

## Overview

NovaCart wants every customer to feel confident purchasing from us. This
policy explains when an order qualifies for a refund, how refunds are
calculated, which payment methods we can refund to, and how long the process
takes. It applies to all purchases made through the NovaCart website or
mobile app unless a product page states a different policy.

## Refund Eligibility

An order is eligible for a full refund when any of the following is true:

1. The item arrives damaged, defective, or not as described.
2. The item is returned unused, in its original packaging, within **30 days**
   of the delivery date.
3. The order was cancelled before it shipped (see `cancellation_policy.md`
   for cancellation-specific rules).
4. NovaCart shipped the wrong item.
5. The package is confirmed lost in transit by the carrier and NovaCart
   cannot reship within 10 business days.

Items are **not eligible** for a refund when:

- More than 30 days have passed since delivery, unless the item is still
  under manufacturer warranty and is defective.
- The item shows signs of use beyond simple inspection (for physical goods)
  or has been installed/registered (for software licenses and digital
  activation codes).
- The item is a gift card, store credit, or other stored-value product.
- The item is custom-configured or made-to-order, unless it arrived
  defective.
- Hygiene-sensitive items (e.g., earbuds, grooming devices) have had their
  protective seal removed, unless defective.

## Refund Amount

- **Full refund**: product price + original shipping cost, when NovaCart is
  at fault (damaged, defective, wrong item, lost package).
- **Partial refund**: product price minus original outbound shipping cost,
  when the customer changes their mind and returns an eligible, unused item.
- **Restocking consideration**: large appliances and bulky items may have a
  restocking fee of up to 10%, always disclosed on the product page before
  purchase. NovaCart never applies an undisclosed fee.
- Promotional discounts are refunded proportionally. If an order used a
  buy-one-get-one offer and only one item is returned, the refund reflects
  the discounted price actually paid for that item, not its full list price.

## How to Request a Refund

1. Sign in to your NovaCart account and open **Order History**.
2. Select the order and choose **Request Refund**.
3. Select a reason from the list (damaged, wrong item, no longer needed,
   defective, other) and add a short description.
4. If the item must be shipped back, NovaCart generates a prepaid return
   label for eligible cases (see below).
5. Submit the request. You will receive a confirmation email with a
   reference number.

Refund requests can also be started by contacting support through the
in-app chat or by describing the issue in the NovaCart complaint form, which
routes automatically to the refunds team.

## Prepaid Return Labels

A prepaid return label is provided at no cost when:

- The item is damaged, defective, or incorrect (NovaCart's fault).
- The customer is a NovaCart Plus member (any reason, within 30 days).

For standard change-of-mind returns without NovaCart Plus, a return
shipping fee of a flat $4.99 is deducted from the refund unless the order
total was over $75, in which case the label is free.

## Processing Time

| Step | Typical duration |
|---|---|
| Return item received at warehouse | 2–7 business days after drop-off (varies by carrier) |
| Inspection and approval | 1–2 business days after receipt |
| Refund issued to original payment method | 1 business day after approval |
| Funds visible in customer account | 3–10 business days, depending on the bank or card issuer |

For refunds that do not require a physical return (e.g., a lost package or
a digital order), the refund is typically issued within 2 business days of
approval.

## Refund Method

Refunds are always returned to the original payment method:

- **Credit/debit card**: refunded to the same card. If the card has expired
  or been cancelled, the customer's bank generally still routes the funds to
  a replacement card or account linked to the same cardholder; if that
  fails, NovaCart issues store credit as a fallback and notifies the
  customer.
- **Digital wallet** (e.g., PayPal-style wallet): refunded to the wallet
  balance.
- **Store credit or gift card used at checkout**: refunded as store credit.
- **Split payments** (partly gift card, partly card): refunded proportionally
  to each method in the order it was charged.

NovaCart cannot redirect a refund to a different card, bank account, or
person than the one used for the original purchase. This rule protects
customers from fraudulent refund-redirection attempts.

## Order Not Yet Delivered

If a refund is requested before the order has shipped, NovaCart cancels the
order instead of processing a post-delivery refund, and the full amount
(including shipping) is returned. If the order has already shipped but not
yet arrived, the customer can either wait for delivery and start a standard
return, or refuse the delivery, after which the refund is processed once the
carrier confirms the return-to-sender scan.

## Refunds for Failed or Duplicate Payments

If a customer is charged twice for the same order (a duplicate transaction
caused by a payment gateway retry), NovaCart automatically detects and
refunds the duplicate charge within 3 business days, without requiring a
support request. If the customer notices it first, they can flag it through
`payment_faq.md`'s dispute process or contact support directly.

## Escalation

If a refund is denied and the customer believes this is incorrect, or if a
refund has not arrived within the timeframes above, the case should be
escalated according to `escalation_policy.md`. Support agents must never
promise a refund timeline shorter than what is stated in this document, and
must never claim a refund has been completed when it has only been
approved.

## Worked Examples

**Example 1 — Change of mind, no membership.** A customer buys a $60
speaker with Standard shipping ($0, order qualified for free shipping) and
decides after two weeks they don't want it. The item is unused and in
original packaging. Because the order was over $75 is false here (order
was $60), the $4.99 return label fee applies, and the refund is $60.00
minus the $4.99 label fee, minus nothing else since no promotional
discount was used. Total refund: $55.01, processed to the original card.

**Example 2 — Damaged item, NovaCart Plus member.** A NovaCart Plus member
receives a router with a cracked case. Because the item is damaged
(NovaCart's fault) *and* the customer is a Plus member, the return label
is free either way. The refund is the full product price plus the original
shipping cost, with no restocking fee, since restocking fees only apply to
large appliances and bulky items and only when disclosed on the product
page.

**Example 3 — Partial multi-item return.** A customer orders three
accessories in one $90 order with free shipping (order was over $50) and
returns one $20 accessory because they no longer need it. Since the
remaining two items still total $70 (still over the $50 free-shipping
threshold), no shipping recalculation is needed. The refund is $20.00
minus a proportional share of the return label fee if a shared label
was used, or the flat $4.99 if a dedicated label was issued for that item.

**Example 4 — Promotional discount return.** A customer uses a
buy-one-get-one 50%-off promotion on two $40 items (paying $40 + $20 =
$60 total) and returns the second item, the one that was discounted. The
refund reflects the $20 actually paid for that item, not its $40 list
price, consistent with the rule that promotional discounts are refunded
proportionally to the amount actually paid.

## Frequently Misunderstood Points

- **"30 days" is measured from delivery, not from purchase.** An order
  that shipped slowly still gets the full 30 days starting from the day it
  arrived, not the day it was placed.
- **A refund being "approved" is not the same as a refund being
  "completed."** Approval starts the 1-business-day-to-issue window; the
  money is not yet in the customer's account at the moment of approval.
- **Digital wallets and gift cards refund to themselves, never to cash.**
  A gift card used at checkout is refunded as store credit even if the
  customer would prefer cash back, because NovaCart cannot convert
  stored-value balances to cash per the rules in `payment_faq.md`.
- **A restocking fee is never a surprise.** If a product page does not
  disclose a restocking fee before purchase, none applies to that order,
  regardless of item category.
- **Refusing delivery is not the same as cancelling.** A refused package
  still has to be scanned as "return to sender" by the carrier before a
  refund can start; simply telling support "I refused it" does not by
  itself trigger the refund.

## Refunds and Warranty Claims

Refunds under this policy are separate from **warranty replacements**
described in `technical_support.md` and `router_troubleshooting.md`. A
product that develops a hardware fault after the 30-day window is not
eligible for a refund under this document — it is eligible for a warranty
replacement instead, which follows a different process (prepaid return of
the faulty unit, replacement ships once the return is scanned). Support
should not tell a customer outside the 30-day window that they are
entitled to a refund; the correct path is the warranty process.
